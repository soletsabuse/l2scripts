How to use

Files

  • .zmap zone (Library\Zones) — becomes the starting navmesh
  • .navtrace run — a cyan guide line (and its farm spots)
  • .navgrid — the map with every setting, the rooms, farm spots and the safe point
  • .txt script — its corners and safe point; an old script can also hand its settings to Settings
  • Open or drop the files anywhere on the page; Save writes the .navgrid

Painting

  • Pick Walkable or Wall and paint with the Brush or Fill (a click inside a shape, like in Paint; the run lines help as walls)
  • Then press Rebuild and check it with Route A→B — the orange line is exactly how the bot runs
  • Cell — the navmesh cell size; a new size moves the painting onto the new cells

Map objects

  • Room — click the corners, Enter or the first corner closes it, drag a corner to move it; room settings — the panel on the right
  • Spot — a farm spot: click to place, drag the star to move
  • Safe — the safe point: click to place, drag to move
  • Node — an own graph node the bot runs through too
  • Rail — points in order, Enter to finish: the bot runs it point by point without cutting (for places where it catches pillars / corners). Started next to the teleport landing point (within 800) — it is the way to the spot after the teleport, the place needs no green
  • Erase — click a node, rail, farm spot, the safe point or a room corner

View & keys

  • Wheel — zoom · right mouse or Space + drag — pan
  • Ctrl+Z — undo · Ctrl+S — save the .navgrid
  • Enter / Esc / Backspace — finish / cancel / remove the last point of a room or rail
  • Level — the map tile layer · Offset — shift of the map tiles
